## What does this actually mean for your workflow?

Think of Event Templates as living blueprints. Instead of mindlessly duplicating raw events, you save an event once into your template library (doors, shopkeepers, torches, save points, treasure chests...) and then you just plop them onto your maps whenever you need them.

Here is the magical part: when you tweak the original template, every single copy across your whole game updates automatically. Change the sound effect or adjust a dialog line in the template, and boom, all 40 chests on 10 different maps are updated instantly. No hunting through map trees, no tedious manual edits.

## But what if I need one event to be a little different?

We know games aren't made of identical clones, so we built what we call granular overrides right into the system.

Say you have a template for a treasure chest, but this specific chest in the dragon's lair should drop a legendary sword instead of 50 gold. You can just open that chest and edit the reward command. RPG Weaver is smart enough to remember only the things you explicitly changed. That means your chest keeps its unique legendary loot, but if you later decide to update the chest's sound effect or opening graphic in the master template, it will still pull in those changes seamlessly!

If you ever change your mind and want it back to normal, a quick click on Reset to Source wipes your custom tweaks and snaps it right back in sync. And if an event ends up evolving into a full-blown custom story cutscene, you can simply duplicate it or delete the master template, and all your instances safely persist as fully functional, independent events.
